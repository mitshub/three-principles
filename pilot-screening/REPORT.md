## Definitions and thresholds

- **Repeat point:** a `wpdx_id` with ≥2 distinct dated keys in `water_point_history`. **Span** is first to last date, in years (days / 365.25).
- **Water-qualifying unit:** ≥ **30** repeat points with span ≥ **3** years (`MIN_REPEAT_POINTS`, `MIN_SPAN_YEARS` in `02_boundaries_wpdx.py`).
- **Violence-qualifying unit:** ≥ **3** UCDP GED 26.1 events with `where_prec` = 1, in ≥ **2** distinct years, within **2010–2025** (`MIN_EVENTS`, `MIN_YEARS`, `YEAR_FROM`, `YEAR_TO` in `03_ucdp.py`). The codebook defines `where_prec` 1 as "exact location of the event known and coded".
- **Unit assignment:** a point-in-polygon join (`within`) against geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM1 and ADM2, full-resolution GeoJSON. ADM1 and ADM2 are counted separately, each on its own layer. UCDP events are joined by their coordinates, whatever their GED `country` value.
- **Country-level UCDP share:** uses the GED field `country`, which the codebook defines as "Name of the country in which the event takes place". ISO3 codes are mapped to GED names through an explicit table in `03_ucdp.py`. That table maps Zimbabwe to `Zimbabwe (Rhodesia)` and Eswatini to `Kingdom of eSwatini (Swaziland)`. **Malawi does not occur in GED 26.1 at all.**

### What these numbers show

- **One ADM2 unit in all 19 countries meets both thresholds:** South Gonder, Ethiopia.
- At ADM1 there are 7 such units, in 6 countries.
- Most of the ADM1 overlaps are very large units. Amhara is 153,195 km², and Bihār and West Bengal are each more than 85,000 km².
- In three of the overlap units, the violence side barely clears the threshold:
  - Montserrado: 3 events in 3 years.
  - Northern Bahr el Ghazal: 4 events in 4 years.
  - West Bengal: 3 distinct years.
- Bihār clears the water threshold with exactly 30 repeat points.
- **The two sides rarely coincide.**
  - Countries with many repeat points have almost no precise GED events in 2010–2025: Liberia 4, Sierra Leone 1, Zambia 0, Uganda 43.
  - Countries with many precise events have almost no long repeat series: Nigeria 31 repeat points spanning ≥3 years out of 99,245 points; Central African Rep. 5; Bangladesh 0.

### What these numbers do not show

- **Whether repeat points and events are near each other within a unit.** This screen counts both sides inside the same polygon. In a 153,195 km² region, both thresholds can be met with no water point anywhere near an event.
- **Whether the timing lines up.** Event years are restricted to 2010–2025, but water spans are not restricted by date. The most common first→last year pairs among repeat points spanning ≥3 years (`first_last_year_pairs_span_ge_3y_top5`, step 1) show this:
  - **South Sudan:** 2005→2016 (155 points) and 1998→2002 (14). Many South Sudan pairs predate the event window.
  - **Ethiopia:** 2008→2020, 2009→2020 and 2011→2020.
  - **Uganda:** 2010→2014 and 2010→2015.
  - **India:** 2016→2019 and 2015→2019.
  - **Liberia:** 2011→2017 (2,141 points).
  - **Haiti:** 2016→2020 and 2015→2020.
- **Comparable units across countries.** "ADM1" means 4 regions in Uganda, Sierra Leone and Eswatini, 3 regions in Malawi, 47 counties in Kenya and 36 states/UTs in India. So ADM1 counts are not comparable between countries.
- **Violence below the UCDP threshold.** The GED 26.1 codebook describes the dataset as "tracing the events of all UCDP conflict dyads […] for both active years (years that have crossed the 25 battle related deaths threshold) and non- active years". It covers state-based, non-state and one-sided violence. Zero GED events does not mean zero violence. ACLED was not used (see Blocked).
- **Water functionality.** The outcome field is `status_id`; see the caveats below.

The "Pairs ≥3 y apart" count is a count of consecutive pairs, not of points. A point with three records can contribute two pairs. That is why it can exceed "span ≥3 y", as in Uganda and Kenya.

**`status_id` caveats** (the same as in the Kenya audit; the field descriptions are quoted from the WPdx+ metadata):
- `status_id` is "Identify if any water is available on the day of the visit, recognizing that it may be a limited flow." It measures water on the visit day, not mechanical functionality. It is the only status field stored in `water_point_history`.
- Some records have `status_id` = `Unknown`. These pairs are excluded from Yes→No and No→Yes but included in the pair totals. Counts per country are in `status_checks.history_status_id_values`; for example, Nigeria has 13,060 and Sierra Leone 7,869.
- A pair may span two different sources with different survey methods. Source splits for each pair were analysed only for Kenya, in `pilot-kenya/`.
- **`status_clean` has the Kenya problem in every country.** In the latest row, rows with an empty raw `status` are coded `status_clean = "Non-Functional"` in all 19 countries. The only exceptions are 1 row in Uganda (10,386 of 10,387) and 4 in Ghana (11,280 of 11,284). Examples:
  - Ethiopia 23,288 rows; Bangladesh 18,234; Haiti 6,276, which is every row.
  - Many "Non-Functional" rows have `status_id` = Yes: Ethiopia 21,377, Ghana 16,050, Bangladesh 13,954, Uganda 9,937.
  - `status_clean` should not be used as an outcome in any of these countries.
- `wpdx_id` linkage is WPdx's own clustering. The Kenya proximity sensitivity check found repeat counts depend heavily on the linkage rule. That check was not repeated for other countries.

  - Ethiopia ADM1 and ADM2: 2016.
  - Central African Rep. ADM1: 2006.
  - Uganda ADM2: 2006.
  - Rwanda ADM2: 2012.
  - We did not check whether units created later are missing.
